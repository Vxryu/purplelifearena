    Controls
    • WeaponsDeathmatch: 1 rifle, 2 sniper (one shot, slow reload between shots), 3 knife (2 hits, 1 from behind). Right click aims; with the knife it throws it. Enter opens the global chat.
    • MoveWASD (ZQSD on a French keyboard), Shift or Left Ctrl to run, Space to jump. If your browser closes the tab with Ctrl+W, press W first then Ctrl, or play in fullscreen (F).
    • ShootLeft click, hold for full auto. R reloads. Headshots deal double.
    • DashE, a quick burst (not in Parkour). Low crates and barriers can be jumped on.
    • ParkourHold Space to bunny hop. In the air, turn the mouse and hold A or D to gain speed. R restarts the level, N and B switch to the next or previous level.
